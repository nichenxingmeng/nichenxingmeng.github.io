I am a first-year PhD student at the [Institute for Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Technology (INSAIT)](https://insait.ai/), advised by [Prof. Luc Van Gool](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=TwMib_QAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Dr. Jinjin Gu](https://www.jasongt.com/). I received my M.S. in Computer Technology from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) in 2025, under the supervision of [Prof. Xinfeng Zhang](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~csxfzhang) and worked closely with [Prof. Ruiqin Xiong](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=46Rur-YAAAAJ&hl=en) of Peking University. I was also fortunate to supervised by [Dr. Li Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8G5-2OMAAAAJ&hl=en) and to collaborate with [Dr. Shijie Zhao](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shijie-zhao-bb017a110/) at ByteDance Inc. Prior to that, I earned my B.E. in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing, and Automation from Tongji University in 2022.

My research focuses on **low-level vision**, including image/video generation, super-resolution, and restoration.
